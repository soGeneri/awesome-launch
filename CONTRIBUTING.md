# Contributing

Thanks for helping make this list better. Its purpose is to point founders to resources the community actually recommends, so the rules below are strict.

## No self-promotion

**Pull requests that add a product, site, tool, or project you build, run, work for, or are paid to promote will be closed without review.** Disclosing the affiliation doesn't change that.

This includes:

- Launch boards, product directories, and "Product Hunt alternatives" submitted by their own makers
- Entries added mainly to get a backlink, a dofollow link, or "domain authority"
- Listings that require a badge, a backlink, or payment to be included
- Submissions made on behalf of a client or employer
- Repeat submissions of the same site under different accounts or sections

If your product is genuinely useful, someone else will suggest it.

## What gets accepted

An entry should be:

- **Established**: around for a reasonable time with a real, active user base, not launched in the last few months
- **Independently recommended**: you use it and you have no stake in it
- **Relevant**: it helps people launch, grow, fund, or learn about a product, and it fits an existing section
- **Not a duplicate**: check the list and open PRs first

Low-effort entries are also closed. That covers thin AI-tool directories, new projects with little to show yet, and spammy descriptions.

## How to submit

1. Add **one** entry per pull request, in the most relevant section.
2. Match the existing format:
   ```md
   - **[Name](https://example.com/)** – Short, factual description.
   ```
3. Keep the description neutral and factual, with no marketing language or SEO claims.
4. In the PR description, briefly say why you recommend it and confirm that you aren't affiliated with it.

Fixes for broken links, typos, and outdated entries are always welcome.

The maintainer has final say on what's included.
